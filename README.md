# El Pollo Loco 🐔🎮

A browser-based jump-and-run game where Pepe fights dangerous chickens, collects bottles and coins, and battles the final boss at the end of the journey.

## About the Project

El Pollo Loco is a 2D jump-and-run browser game developed with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The player controls Pepe through different challenges, avoids enemies, collects items, and fights against dangerous killer chickens. Enemies can be defeated either by jumping on them or by throwing collected bottles. At the end of the level, a final boss awaits and can only be defeated with strategy and timing.

The goal is to survive, defeat the enemies, and win the final battle.

## Features

- Jump-and-run gameplay
- Character movement and animations
- Collectable bottles and coins
- Throwing mechanics with bottles
- Enemy interactions
- Final boss battle
- Health and game status system
- Win / lose conditions
- Sound effects and animations

## Gameplay

- Move Pepe through the level
- Collect bottles to attack enemies
- Jump on killer chickens to defeat them
- Avoid losing health from enemy attacks
- Fight and defeat the end boss at the end of the map
- Win the game or lose if your health reaches zero
